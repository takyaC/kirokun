# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: July 13, 2026**

**Last Updated: October 15, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how user information is handled in the Kirokun app (the “App”) and on the App’s promotional website (the “Website”).

## 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the supported App version, distribution environment and user settings, the App may collect the following information to understand usage and investigate issues:

* Usage times, launches, screens and features used, and types of interactions
* Whether a first record or continued use occurred, and counts of activities, sets or templates (not the values entered in records)
* Technical information such as App version, operating system, device type and language
* App-instance and installation identifiers generated by analytics or diagnostic SDKs
* Diagnostic information such as crash or error categories, code locations and stack traces

These identifiers do not directly contain a name or email address, but may associate usage with the same installation. We therefore do not describe all collected information as completely anonymous or free of identifiers.

---

## 2. Handling of Record Contents

We do not send activity names, titles, memos, comments, category or tag names, user-created template contents, entered text or numerical values, or health data values obtained from HealthKit as usage analytics events. We distinguish these contents from usage information such as record or set counts and whether a feature was used.

For error reporting, the App does not attach record databases, record contents or screenshots, and replaces error messages it handles with fixed categories and code locations. However, automatically generated iOS or library crash reports may contain exception classes and messages that the App cannot filter. We cannot completely rule out unexpected values appearing in those messages.

If you choose to share or export data, it is passed to the destination you select. This is separate from analytics and does not upload your record contents to analytics services.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

* Improve App quality and usability
* Analyze usage and continued-use patterns
* Detect issues, notify the developer, and investigate and resolve problems
* Evaluate and improve features
* Provide the service securely

We do not use this information for advertising or to identify people by their real-world identity.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

### App usage: Aptabase

We use Aptabase to understand screen and feature usage, record creation and continued use, use of official templates, and whether HealthKit integration features are used. We do not send entered record values or health data values. Aptabase does not use cookies, device-specific identifiers or fingerprinting. See the [Aptabase Privacy Policy](https://aptabase.com/legal/privacy).

### App usage: Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4)

In versions that support GA4 and have collection enabled, we use this Google LLC service to analyze permitted screens, interaction types and counts, first records and continued use. Its SDK also processes automatic events such as launches and sessions, device and App information, and an app-instance identifier.

We do not send activity names, entered values or a custom User-ID. HealthKit-related events, health data values and official template identification codes are also excluded from GA4 events. We configure advertising identifier (IDFA) and vendor identifier (IDFV) collection and advertising personalization to be disabled.

### App diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics

We use this Google LLC service to detect crashes and certain handled errors and notify the developer. Alongside error categories and code locations, its SDK processes device and App information, installation identifiers and native crash information. Section 2 explains the limits of filtering automatically generated messages.

When used together with GA4, permitted usage events may appear in Crashlytics reports as a history of interactions preceding an issue.

See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) and [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy?hl=en). Information may be processed and stored on servers outside Japan.

### Promotional Website: Aptabase

The Website uses Aptabase to understand visits and improve its content. It processes page views, App Store link clicks, referring domains, campaign information supplied with visits, and browser or device information. Website analytics is separate from the App's GA4 integration.

---

## 5. IP Addresses and Related Information

Network information such as IP addresses may be processed when the App or Website communicates with analytics and other services. In addition to providing and protecting those services, GA4 may derive approximate locations such as country, region or city from an IP address. This does not involve collecting precise GPS location for analytics.

---

## 6. Stopping Usage Analytics

In supported versions, turn off “Allow usage analytics” under Settings → App Info to stop new Aptabase and GA4 usage collection from that device. Saving and viewing records remain available. If you turn it on again, collection conditions are checked on the next launch.

Turning collection off does not delete data already sent or guarantee cancellation of transmissions already in progress. Crashlytics operates independently for diagnostics and is not stopped by this setting. The setting also does not affect Website analytics.

---

## 7. Data Retention

Usage information is retained as needed for service improvement according to each service's retention settings and specifications. GA4 is configured to retain event-level details for 2 months and user data for 14 months, with the user-data retention period reset by new activity. These settings are distinct from retention of standard aggregated reports.

According to Google, Crashlytics retains crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. See the Firebase information linked above for details.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information, please contact us using the details below.
